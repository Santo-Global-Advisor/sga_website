Twelve guides drawn from Move to Brazil, our 32-chapter relocation book. Each one covers a decision foreigners get wrong often enough that it costs them money — and each one tells you where the line between reading and needing counsel actually sits.

— I

Immigration pathways

Brazil has no single 'move here' visa. It has a menu of pathways, each tied to a purpose. These three cover the routes foreigners actually use.

VITEM XIV · RN 45/2022

The Brazil Digital Nomad Visa: Requirements, Limits and the Tax Trap

Brazil's digital nomad pathway is one of the most flexible immigration mechanisms available to a foreigner today. It is also the one most often mistaken for a tax answer — which it is not.

8 min read → VITEM XIV · RN 40/2019

The Brazil Retirement Visa: US$2,000 a Month, and What Comes After

Brazil's retirement pathway is one of the more predictable routes available to a foreigner — provided the income requirement is clearly documented. What it does not settle is how your pension will be taxed once you live here.

8 min read → RN 36/2018 · Investor residence

The Brazil Investor Visa Through Real Estate: RN 36 in Practice

Buying any property does not, by itself, create a right to residence. The investment has to meet the rule's specific conditions — and the purchase, the financing and the immigration filing have to be coordinated rather than treated as three separate errands.

Tax, registration and money

The area where foreigners most often confuse two entirely separate legal concepts — and where the confusion is most expensive.

Receita Federal · IN SRF 208/2002

Brazilian Tax Residency: The 184-Day Rule Foreigners Miscount

This is the area where foreigners most often confuse two entirely separate legal concepts. Getting the classification wrong — in either direction — can mean paying more than necessary, or under-reporting and facing penalties later.

9 min read → Receita Federal · Registration

CPF for Foreigners: What It Unlocks, and What It Does Not

Without a CPF you can feel invisible to the system — unable to open a bank account, sign a lease, buy property or enrol a child in school. What it is not, however, is any kind of immigration or tax status.

Opening a Bank Account in Brazil as a Foreigner

Your relocation budget should be designed before you move, not reconstructed after your first confusing Brazilian bank statement. Requirements vary by institution, and not every bank serves non-residents at all.

7 min read → Budget · Planning

Cost of Living in Brazil: Why the Number You Read Online Is Wrong

Do not compare Brazil to your home country using a single restaurant meal or a supermarket basket. Compare the entire lifestyle you intend to live here.

A property purchase is not simply finding a house you like and paying the seller. The cheapest part of the transaction is the document review; the expensive part is discovering a problem after the money has moved.

In April 2026 the Supreme Federal Court confirmed the constitutionality of this regime, including its extension to Brazilian companies under foreign-majority control. These rules are not a legacy technicality.
