SJF Immigration & Investments is our partner in Portugal, and the reason we can now answer the question clients actually ask rather than the one we are licensed for.

That question is almost never “how do I move to Brazil.” It is “Portugal or Brazil” — and for years the honest answer was that we could only give them half of it.

Why a partnership rather than an opinion

A Brazilian lawyer is admitted to practise Brazilian law. Portuguese residence rules, the D7 income thresholds, how the Portuguese tax authority treats foreign income — none of that is ours to advise on, and a confident answer from the wrong jurisdiction is worse than no answer.

A question worth asking

When an adviser tells you both countries confidently, ask which bar admitted them, and where. The answer is usually one country, and the other half is guesswork dressed as experience.

SJF works from Lisboa and Funchal across Portugal’s full residence menu — Golden Visa, D7, D8, entrepreneur, highly qualified activity, family reunification — alongside tax and investment advisement, property search and concierge support.

How the two ends fit together

Most cases on this corridor are not a clean choice between two countries. They are a sequence: leave one tax residence, establish another, and decide what happens to the assets that do not move.

A visa answers where you can live. It rarely answers, on its own, what you will owe — and it never answers it for two countries at once.

Both directions are covered, and in both the referring firm stays involved where its own jurisdiction is engaged. A client leaving Brazil for Lisbon still has a Brazilian exit-tax position, Brazilian-situs assets and, often, a Brazilian succession structure that outlives the move. That remains our work. The Portuguese residence application is SJF’s.

Before you make this decision

A referral does not merge the two firms, and it does not change what you pay. Each practice is engaged separately, bills its own scope, and carries its own professional liability. What the partnership changes is coordination — not who is responsible for what.

Where to start

If you already know the destination, start with the firm that covers it. If you do not — which is the more common case — start with a consultation here. The Brazilian analysis is the same either way, and it is the half that determines whether Portugal is worth modelling at all.
