One corridor. Counsel at both ends.

A move between Portugal and Brazil is two legal systems, two tax authorities and two sets of deadlines — not one. We hold the Brazilian end. Our partner holds the Portuguese one.

Clients rarely arrive with a single country in mind. The question is almost never "how do I move to Brazil" — it is "Portugal or Brazil, and what does each one actually cost me."

Answering that honestly requires someone licensed on both sides. Rather than opine on Portuguese law we are not admitted to practise, we built a partnership with a firm that is.

— 01

Heading to Portugal

If your plan points to Lisbon, Porto or Madeira, we introduce you to SJF Immigration & Investments and stay involved on anything with a Brazilian dimension — exit tax position, assets left behind, succession structures that outlive the move.

— 02

Coming to Brazil

SJF's clients who need a Brazilian residence permit, a tax-residence analysis or a corporate structure here are referred to us directly, and arrive with their Portuguese position already documented.
