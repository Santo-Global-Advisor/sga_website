The Brazil Relocation Program.

A single engagement covering residency, legal setup, tax structure and property — managed from first review through arrival and beyond.

01

Visa & residency strategy

Choosing the correct pathway is the single most consequential decision in any relocation. We assess eligibility across every applicable category, compare timelines and conditions, and build the application that gives the strongest case.

Once filed, we manage the process through approval, registration, and first renewal — so nothing lapses in the background.

  • IEligibility assessment across all applicable categories
  • IIDocumentation review, translation and consular handling
  • IIIApplication filing with the Federal Police and consulates
  • IVRegistration (RNM/CRNM) after arrival
  • VRenewal strategy and long-term residency planning
02

CPF & legal registration

The CPF is the foundation of every subsequent action in Brazil — banking, property, contracts, taxes. We obtain it quickly, together with the broader legal identity layer your situation requires.

  • ICPF registration for you and dependents
  • IIDigital certificate (e-CPF) where needed
  • IIIMunicipal and federal registrations relevant to your status
  • IVNotarial powers of attorney when acting remotely
03

Banking & initial setup

Opening a Brazilian bank account as a foreigner remains one of the most friction-prone steps. We select the right institution for your profile, prepare documentation correctly, and coordinate introduction and onboarding.

  • IBank selection based on your profile and objectives
  • IIAccount opening coordination and introduction
  • IIIPix, international transfer and FX setup
  • IVUtilities, mobile line and essential local services
04

Real estate support

Whether renting or buying, Brazilian property transactions carry legal and tax implications that are not obvious to newcomers. We provide full due diligence, contract review, and registration — and if you are buying, structural advice on ownership form.

  • ITitle, liens and registry (matrícula) review
  • IIContract drafting and negotiation
  • IIIOwnership structure — personal, holding or entity
  • IVITBI, notarial and registration coordination
  • VRental contract review and deposit protection
05

Tax structuring & compliance

Brazil's tax system is demanding for residents with foreign income or assets. We define your residency status precisely, structure around it, and keep you compliant year after year.

  • ITax residency analysis and 183-day threshold planning
  • IIForeign income and asset treatment
  • IIICapital gains, rental income and repatriation modelling
  • IVAnnual declaration (DIRPF) and DCBE reporting
  • VDouble-taxation coordination with home jurisdictions
06

Post-arrival support

Moving is not an event, it is a transition. We remain engaged after you arrive — handling the inevitable follow-on matters and staying available as your counsel in Brazil.

  • ISingle point of legal contact in Brazil
  • IIRenewals, registrations and compliance calendar
  • IIIOngoing tax and structural review
  • IVTrusted introductions — accountants, notaries, advisors
